not a toy

built like production software.

crawling the open web is messy and the catalog is read constantly — so it's engineered to feel instant, survive failure, and just run.

fast
  • catalog cached in memory, PostgreSQL-backed
  • stale-while-revalidate on a 2-min TTL
  • one shared engine behind app + MCP
sturdy
  • auth + rate limit (60/min)
  • audio-URL dedup; skip a show after 3 fails
  • oversize audio compressed to fit the 50 MB cap
shipped
  • Docker on a small ~$5/mo VPS
  • heavy lifting lives behind the gateway
  • auto-deployed from main, health-checked

every failure is logged with a reason and streamed to the UI — watch the crawl, and know why something was skipped.
